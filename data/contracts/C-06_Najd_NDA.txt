CONTRACT C-06 — MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
Matter: ORG-1012 (Najd Logistics)
Parties: Najd Logistics and Manar Energy
Language: English

1. Purpose. To exchange confidential information for evaluating a joint bid.
2. Term. Confidentiality obligations last two (2) years from the date of disclosure.
3. Obligations. Each party keeps the other's information confidential and uses it only for the
   Purpose.
4. Return. On request, each party returns or destroys the other's confidential information.
