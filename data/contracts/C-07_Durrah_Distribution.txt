CONTRACT C-07 — DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT
Matter: ORG-1060 (Durrah Retail)
Parties: Durrah Retail (Distributor) and Tharwa Brands (Supplier)
Language: English

1. Term. Two (2) years from 1 April 2026; auto-renews for one-year terms unless either party gives
   60 days' notice.

9. Governing Law. Laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia; arbitration in Riyadh.
12. Signatures. Signed by both parties.
