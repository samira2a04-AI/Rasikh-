# Rasikh — Risk Taxonomy
## Section 3 · Rating a Finding

**Clause 3.1 — High risk.**
Uncapped or unlimited liability; an indemnity with no cap; automatic renewal with a notice
window closing within 30 days; a governing law or forum that conflicts with the client's other
obligations; a missing governing-law clause on a cross-border contract; any Sharia-sensitive
construct (Section 4) left unaddressed.

**Clause 3.2 — Medium risk.**
Capped liability set above the contract value; auto-renewal with a comfortable notice window;
one-sided termination rights; vague scope or deliverables; payment terms longer than 60 days.

**Clause 3.3 — Low risk.**
Standard mutual terms; capped liability at or below contract value; fixed term with clear expiry;
balanced termination rights.

**Clause 3.4 — Every rating cites its clause.**
A risk rating is never given on its own. It names the contract clause that earned it (Rulebook
0.1). A finding the assistant cannot tie to a clause is not rated — it is escalated.
