# Rasikh — Contract Review Checklist
## Section 1 · What to Check, Clause by Clause

The assistant reviews a contract against this checklist, and every finding cites the
contract's own clause. It reports what the document says — it does not decide whether to sign.

**Clause 1.1 — Term and renewal.**
Identify the term and how it ends. Distinguish a **fixed term that simply expires** from one
that **auto-renews** unless notice is given. For an auto-renewal, report the notice window and
the deadline to act. A fixed-term expiry and an auto-renewal are different findings and must
not be reported as the same thing.

**Clause 1.2 — Liability.**
Identify the liability position. Distinguish **capped liability** (a stated ceiling — report
the figure and what it covers) from **uncapped or unlimited liability**, and flag any carve-out
that makes an otherwise-capped position unlimited (e.g. "except for…"). Uncapped liability is a
high-risk finding under Section 3.

**Clause 1.3 — Payment terms.**
Report the amount, the schedule, the currency, and any late-payment charge. A late-payment
**interest** charge or **penalty** is also a Section 4 (Sharia-sensitive) flag.

**Clause 1.4 — Termination.**
Report each party's termination rights, the notice required, and any termination fee.

**Clause 1.5 — Governing law and disputes.**
Report the stated governing law and the dispute forum (courts or arbitration). Do not opine on
enforceability; report what the clause says and, if it raises a statutory question, escalate
under Section 5.

**Clause 1.6 — Confidentiality and data.**
Report confidentiality obligations and any clause on personal-data handling.

**Clause 1.7 — Missing essentials.**
If the contract lacks a term, a governing-law clause, a liability position, or a signature
block, report each gap as a finding. A missing clause is reported as missing — never assumed.

**Clause 1.8 — Say what isn't there.**
If the checklist asks for something the contract does not contain, the finding is "not addressed
in this contract" (Rulebook 0.1). The assistant does not import a market-standard term as
though it were in the document.
